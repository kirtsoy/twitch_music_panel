Twitch Extension Compliance

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 23, 2026 • Official Kirtsoy Ecosystem Extensions
Summary: Kirtsoy Twitch Extensions (including "420 Mixtape Player", "Kirtsoy Telegram Panel", and "My Insta Feed") are built with user privacy as a fundamental standard. We do NOT collect, transmit, store, track, or sell any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) from Twitch viewers.

1. Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to all Twitch extensions developed and operated by Kirill Tsoy (KIRTSOY), specifically:

  • 420 Mixtape Player — Interactive web audio player for the official musical album "420 mixtape (vol. 1)".
  • Kirtsoy Telegram Panel — Public channel news feed and chat preview bridge for Twitch viewers.
  • My Insta Feed — Verified public Instagram stream viewer displaying recent multimedia content.

2. Information Collection and Usage

Our extensions operate strictly in client display mode:

  • No Account Required: Viewers are not required to log in or link external accounts to experience the extensions.
  • No Personal Data: We do not collect usernames, IP addresses, email addresses, Twitch IDs, or financial data.
  • Local Storage: The audio player may store harmless local playback state (such as audio volume, shuffle state, or mute settings) locally in the viewer's browser localStorage. This data never leaves the user's computer.

3. Third-Party Services and CDNs

To deliver optimized streaming media and live synchronization, the extensions connect to trusted, secure endpoints:

  • Twitch Extension Assets: Hosted and served via Twitch's official CDN (ext-twitch.tv).
  • GitHub Pages CDN: Hosts audio files for the 420 Mixtape player via secure HTTPS with HTTP 206 partial range streaming.
  • Cloudflare Workers: Acts as an encrypted serverless proxy for public channel announcements and cached Instagram feeds, preventing direct client exposure of private credentials.

4. Copyright & Audio Ownership

All music, audio stems, graphical covers, artwork, and visual animations presented within the 420 Mixtape Player are 100% originally created, recorded, produced, and owned by Kirill Tsoy (KIRTSOY). Kirill Tsoy holds full master recording and publishing rights, granting perpetual, non-exclusive rights for interactive broadcast playback on Twitch.

5. Children's Privacy

Our extensions are intended for general Twitch audiences and do not knowingly solicit or collect data from children under the age of 13.
